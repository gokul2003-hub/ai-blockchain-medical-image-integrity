## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a serene, authoritative, and elevated scientific editorial aesthetic tailored for clinical researchers, forensic pathologists, and diagnostic integrity specialists. Rejecting the cliched cyberpunk tropes of dark-mode neon and aggressive security dashboards, the system instead evokes the quiet confidence of high-tier peer-reviewed medical publications, precision optical instruments, and state-of-the-art diagnostic suites.

Visual attributes balance warm, tactile ivory surfaces with surgically defined micro-borders, deep carbon typography, and balanced botanical-clinical accents. The emotional resonance is calm, uncompromisingly precise, and deeply trustworthy—bridging the gap between cutting-edge neural forensics and certified clinical reliability.

## Colors
The palette balances organic serenity with clinical exactitude across light-dominant surfaces.

- **Primary Clinical Teal (`#2E6F7E`, `#438A9B`)**: The anchor of technical authority, used for active navigational states, primary visual focal points, and core telemetry metrics.
- **Secondary Sage Green (`#3E6B56`, `#4F8069`)**: Reserved for verified operational actions, confirmed image authenticity indicators, and successful forensic recovery events.
- **Tertiary AI Forensics Lavender (`#7C6EB0`, `#9182C7`, background tint `#EDEBF5`)**: Dedicated specifically to machine learning inferences, neural layer overlays, metadata anomaly tags, and model confidence scores.
- **Neutral Ivory Canvas (`#FAF8F5`, `#F5F2EB`, border tints `#E8E5DF`, `#D5D1C8`)**: Warms the clinical viewport, preventing eye fatigue during prolonged analytical sessions while ensuring maximum legibility.
- **Alert Coral Crimson (`#D35B51`, light tint `#FBEBEA`)**: Strictly functional. Never used decoratively; reserved exclusively for verified cryptographic tampering, file corruption, and critical diagnostic discrepancies.
- **Deep Carbon Typography (`#1C2024`, `#2A2F35`, secondary `#4B525B`)**: Replaces pure black to retain editorial warmth while meeting strict contrast ratios.

## Typography
Typographic rules are divided between display hierarchy and operational clarity:

- **Headlines (Manrope)**: Geometric, balanced, and confident. Used in titles, metric banners, and module labels to create an authoritative scientific atmosphere.
- **Body & Data Displays (Public Sans)**: Highly neutral and engineered for maximum institutional clarity. Tabular numerals (`tnum`) must be enforced on all metric tables, cryptographic hashes, and DICOM coordinate feeds.
- **Scale and Rhythm**: All vertical metrics adhere strictly to fractional rem sizing. Titles leverage tight negative letter-spacing for structural coherence, while technical micro-labels feature tracked small-caps or uppercase transformations for instant legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column modular grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single- or dual-column stream on mobile devices.

- **Desktop Workspace (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column structure with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` margins. The workspace accommodates persistent triage sidebars (3 columns), a high-contrast viewport (6 columns), and a real-time forensics inspector (3 columns).
- **Tablet / Clinical Cart (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Side panels convert to tabbed drawers or sliding sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Data panels reflow into vertically stacked collapsible cards.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing units follow an exact 4px/8px incremental base. Gaps between related forensic metadata fields are constrained to `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while top-level analytical zones preserve generous negative space via `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to eliminate cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through structured tonal layering and fine micro-borders rather than heavy blur-radius drop shadows.

- **Flat Ivory Foundation**: The page canvas is rendered in `#FAF8F5`. Primary analytical containers and functional cards sit on `#FFFFFF` or elevated `#F5F2EB`.
- **Micro-Borders**: Hierarchy is demarcated by crisp 1px borders using `#E8E5DF` for standard modular dividers and `#D5D1C8` for high-priority interactive components.
- **Subtle Elevation**: Floating modals, flyout menus, and active DICOM overlays apply a faint, organic ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(28, 32, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 32, 36, 0.02)`.
- **Specialized Imaging Viewport**: Medical imaging canvases break the ivory paradigm with an isolated, edge-bounded `#0D1117` neutral dark container to ensure zero optical bias when inspecting radiographic pixel integrity.

## Shapes
The shape language is disciplined and tactile, utilizing `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius) for standard interface containers, data modules, and input fields.

- **Base Cards and Panels**: `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii combined with 1px micro-borders preserve a sharp, clinical laboratory presence.
- **Large Overlays and Modals**: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px) provides an intentional, soft edge transition without softening into playful curves.
- **Pill Badges & AI Status Indicators**: Pill shapes (`9999px` radius) are used selectively for contextual metadata tags, status pills, and forensics chips, contrasting cleanly against the crisp rectangular grid.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Verified / Confirm)**: Sage green (`#3E6B56`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` radius, 1px solid `#345948`. Hover shifts to `#4F8069`.
- **Clinical Accent Action (Run Diagnostic)**: Primary clinical teal (`#2E6F7E`) background, white text, 1px solid `#255B67`. Hover shifts to `#438A9B`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px micro-border in `#D5D1C8`, deep charcoal text (`#2A2F35`). Hover transitions to `#F5F2EB`.
- **Destructive / Tamper Resolution**: `#D35B51` background, white text. Hover shifts to `#BE4E45`.

### Chips & Forensic Status Pills
- **AI Forensics Tag**: Soft lavender background (`#EDEBF5`), border in `rgba(124, 110, 176, 0.25)`, text in `#7C6EB0`. Fully pill-shaped with `0.25rem 0.625rem` padding.
- **Authenticity Verified Tag**: Soft sage tint (`#EBF2EE`), border in `rgba(62, 107, 86, 0.25)`, text in `#3E6B56`.
- **Tamper Alert Badge**: Coral tint (`#FBEBEA`), border in `rgba(211, 91, 81, 0.3)`, text in `#D35B51`, bold micro-typography (`label-sm`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text & Numeric Inputs**: Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`), border in `#E8E5DF`, charcoal text (`#1C2024`), placeholder in `#4B525B`. `0.25rem` radius. Focus ring utilizes a crisp 1px stroke of `#2E6F7E` with no excessive glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 1px micro-border `#D5D1C8`, checked state filled with `#2E6F7E` featuring a sharp white checkmark.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a single 1px line in `#E8E5DF`. Padding is standardized to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- Header bars within cards are separated by a fine horizontal rule (`#F5F2EB`) with metric indicators set in `Public Sans` tabular figures.

### Dedicated Medical Imaging Panels
- Isolated viewports feature a deep neutral matte black (`#0D1117`) internal canvas framed by `#E8E5DF` outer panel walls.
- Floating overlay controls (zoom, pan, forensic heatmaps) are housed in translucent `#FAF8F5` HUD panels with 1px `#D5D1C8` borders and high-contrast charcoal iconography.
